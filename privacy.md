# GradeGebra Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 2026

GradeGebra is an app for planning and tracking grades, published by GladTides ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the app does with information.

## The short version

- Your grades and courses stay on your device.
- GradeGebra has no accounts and no sign-in.
- GradeGebra does not show ads and does not use analytics or tracking.
- We do not sell your information.

## Information stored on your device

GradeGebra stores the programs, terms, courses, assessments, grading rules and reminder preferences you enter in the app's local storage on your device. We do not receive this information, and we cannot see it.

## Purchases

Apple processes all App Store purchases. GradeGebra uses RevenueCat, a subscription service, to show purchase options and to check whether Pro features are active. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID and information about your purchases, and may receive technical data such as your IP address. It is not linked to a GradeGebra account, because there are none. See the privacy policies of RevenueCat (https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy) and Apple (https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## Reminders

If you turn on assessment reminders, GradeGebra schedules notifications on your device. They are created locally and are not sent through our servers. You can turn them off in the app's Settings, or in your iPhone's notification settings.

## Exports and backups

GradeGebra creates a PDF transcript or a backup file only when you choose to. The file is handed to the app or location you pick, such as Files, Mail or Messages. After that, it is controlled by the place you shared it with, not by GradeGebra.

## Deleting your information

Go to Settings, then Delete all data, to remove the programs, terms, courses and assessments saved in GradeGebra. This does not delete backup files or PDFs you saved or shared earlier, and it does not remove purchase records held by Apple and RevenueCat. To ask about purchase records, contact us.

## Children

GradeGebra is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The effective date above will change when we do.

## Contact

Questions about this policy: gradegebra@scanzest.uk

## Disclaimer

Results in GradeGebra are estimates. Grading rules vary by institution. Confirm with your institution.
