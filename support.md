# GradeGebra Support

Need help? Email **gradegebra@scanzest.uk**.

When you write, please include your iPhone model, iOS version and the GradeGebra version (Settings, then About). Please do not send your grades or any personal information.

## Frequently asked questions

**Does GradeGebra need an account?**
No. Your data stays on your device.

**My results differ from my university's. Why?**
Grading rules vary by institution, and presets in GradeGebra are starter examples. Check your university's regulations, and use a custom scale or the rounding setting to match them. Results are estimates, not official transcripts.

**How do I restore my purchase?**
Open Settings in GradeGebra and tap Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple account you bought with.

**How do I cancel my subscription?**
Subscriptions are managed by Apple. Open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions. You can also use Manage Subscription in GradeGebra's Settings.

**What happens if my Pro subscription ends?**
Your data stays readable and you can keep editing it and exporting a backup. Adding more terms or courses than the free plan allows needs Pro.

**How do I back up my data or move it to a new phone?**
Use Settings, then Export backup, and save the file somewhere safe. On the new phone, import it with Import backup.

**How do I delete my data?**
Settings, then Delete all data. This cannot be undone, so export a backup first if you want one.

**My reminders did not arrive.**
Check that notifications are allowed for GradeGebra in your iPhone's Settings, that reminders are turned on in the app, and that the assessment has a due date and no score yet.

**Where can I read the privacy policy?**
See the [Privacy Policy](/gradegebra-legal/privacy/).
